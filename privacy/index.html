What data we collect

We collect only what we need to answer you and to sell vehicles:

  • Enquiry details — your name, phone number, email address, the message you send us and the vehicle you asked about. With each form we also record the IP address and browser it was sent from, to protect against spam and abuse.
  • Viewing bookings — the car, the day and time you chose and whether you would like a test drive.
  • Part-exchange requests — your vehicle's registration, make, model, year, mileage, condition, service history and whether there is finance outstanding on it.
  • Car-finder requests — the kind of car you are looking for (make, model, budget, mileage, year, fuel, gearbox, body type) so we can tell you when one arrives.
  • Sales records — where you buy a vehicle, the information needed for the sale, finance introduction, V5C registration and our legal record-keeping obligations.
  • Technical data — a short-lived, one-way hash derived from your IP address and browser, the pages you viewed and the site that referred you. We do not store your raw IP address against your browsing.
  • Social media interactions — if you message us on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp, we see the information that platform shows us, and we may note which platform an enquiry came from.

We do not ask for special category data, and we do not knowingly collect data about children.

Contact forms and enquiries

When you submit an enquiry form we store your name, contact details, message, the vehicle you enquired about and the source of the enquiry so the right person can call you back. That record sits in our own dealership system and is used to answer you — not to build a marketing profile.

Viewing bookings, part-exchange valuations and car-finder requests are handled the same way. A car-finder request stays open so we can contact you when a matching car comes in — ask us at any time and we will close and delete it.

When a form is submitted, our team is alerted by email or a workplace messaging tool containing the details you entered, so we can reply quickly. If you give us a registration for a part exchange, we may check it against the DVLA vehicle record (only the registration number is sent to the DVLA).

If you ask us to stop contacting you, tell us at [insert contact email] and we will.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no third-party cookie banner because there are no third-party cookies to consent to.

  • Strictly necessary cookies — a session cookie is set only when a member of staff signs in to the private admin area. It is required for that area to work and is exempt from consent requirements.
  • No analytics cookies — visit measurement is done server-side without storing an identifier in your browser.

The site also uses your browser's own storage, which never leaves your device on its own:

  • Saved cars — when you tap the heart on a car, its reference is kept in local storage so your shortlist is there next time. Clear it by removing the cars or clearing your browser data.
  • Where you came from — if you arrive from one of our social media links, the platform name (for example “tiktok”) is kept in session storage so an enquiry you send later is credited to that platform. It contains no identifier and is deleted when you close the tab.

Website analytics

We record which pages and vehicles are viewed so we know what stock to buy. Each event stores a salted hash of your IP address and browser that changes every day, which lets us count visitors without identifying you or tracking you over time. Raw IP addresses are not stored alongside browsing activity, and nothing is shared with an advertising network.

Social media integrations

We publish vehicles to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube using each platform's official API. When we post, we send the vehicle photos, video and caption — never customer data.

We read back post statistics (views, likes, comments and shares) so we can tell which platform sells cars. These are aggregate numbers about our posts, not about you individually.

If you interact with us on those platforms, that platform is the controller of the data it holds about you, under its own privacy policy. Links to our profiles open the platform directly; we do not embed their tracking scripts on this site.

How we protect it

  • The dealership admin area requires a password and is not indexed by search engines.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes — never in readable form.
  • Sessions are held in server-side records with short expiry and secure cookies.
  • Authorisation tokens for social media accounts are encrypted at rest and are never exposed to the website front end.
  • Changes to vehicles, prices and sales are recorded in an audit log.
  • Sign-in attempts and enquiry submissions are rate limited.
