Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the things people ask us most. Anything else — just get in touch.

Can I see a car before I travel?

Yes. Most of our cars have a walkaround video on TikTok, and we are happy to send extra photos or a video of anything you want a closer look at — just ask on WhatsApp or by phone.

How do I book a viewing?

Open the car you are interested in and choose a day and time under "Book a Viewing". We will call or text to confirm. You can also just ring us.

Browse cars

Can I take a test drive?

Tick "I would like a test drive" when you book a viewing and bring your driving licence. We will talk you through anything else we need when we confirm.

Do you take part exchanges?

Yes. Send us your registration, mileage and a note on condition and we will come back with a figure, usually the same day. The figure is subject to the car matching the description when you bring it in.

Get a valuation

I have finance outstanding on my car. Can I still part exchange it?

Often, yes. Get a settlement figure from your lender and tell us about it on the part-exchange form so we can work out the numbers with you.

I can't see the car I want. Can you find one?

Tell us what you are looking for — make, budget, mileage, body type — and we will let you know when something that fits comes in.

Use the car finder

Can I reserve a car?

Speak to us about the car you want and we will explain how reserving it works. If a car shows "Reserved", someone is already ahead of you — but it is worth asking to be told if it becomes available again.

Is finance available?

Please ask us about the specific car. We will only discuss finance options we are able to offer, and we will never quote you a figure on the website that is not a real offer.

Do your cars come with a warranty?

Cover depends on the car. Ask us about the car you are interested in and we will tell you exactly what is included before you commit.

What are my rights when I buy a used car from a dealer?

When you buy from a business, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 applies: the car must be of satisfactory quality (taking its age and mileage into account), fit for purpose and as described. Citizens Advice has a clear guide if you want the detail.

What does ULEZ Ready mean on a listing?

It means the car is listed as meeting the emissions standard for London’s Ultra Low Emission Zone — generally Euro 4 for petrol and Euro 6 for diesel. Rules differ between clean air zones, so use the official vehicle checker for any zone you drive in regularly.

How is my personal information used?

Only to answer your enquiry, arrange a viewing or handle a part-exchange or car-finder request. Our privacy policy explains what we keep, for how long and how to ask us to delete it.
